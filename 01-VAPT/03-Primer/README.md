- Practice reconnaissance and enumeration.
- Understand exposed services and potential vulnerabilities.
- Document evidence and security findings.
- Learn appropriate remediation techniques.

## Methodology

1. Identify the authorized lab scope.
2. Perform reconnaissance.
3. Enumerate available services.
4. Analyze potential vulnerabilities.
5. Validate findings within the lab.
6. Document results and remediation recommendations.
